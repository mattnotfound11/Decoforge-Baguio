The catalog

Standing designs we build regularly. Every one can be resized, refinished, or reworked in a different wood — add pieces to your quote list and tell us what to change.

How a commission runs

Four steps, roughly six weeks from first message to delivery.

1

Quote

Send your list or a photo of the space. We reply within two business days with a costed drawing.

2

Sample

You approve a finish sample and joinery detail in person or by courier before we cut stock.

3

Build

Fabrication takes two to five weeks. You get progress photos at frame-up and at finishing.

4

Deliver

Blanket-wrapped delivery and placement across Baguio and Benguet; freight elsewhere in Luzon.

The workshop

Decoforge is eleven people: four joiners, two finishers, a welder, two weavers, and the two of us who draw. We buy lumber from salvage and storm-fall permits in Benguet and Ifugao, and we publish the source of every board on the invoice. If a piece fails on us within five years, we repair it at cost.
